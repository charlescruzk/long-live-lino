# LONG LIVE LINO: photo order (oldest first)
#
# HOW TO EDIT
#   Move lines up or down to change the order.
#   Put a "-" at the start of a line to leave that photo out. Remove the "-" to bring it back.
#   A line starting with "+" sits next to the line above it (in the slideshow they play one after the other).
#   "== ..." lines are chapters. Add "| slow" after a chapter name to give its photos more time.
#   Add "hold" at the end of a photo line to give that one photo extra time.
#   focus: which face is Lino (numbers on the REVIEW sheet), or x,y (0 to 1), or c = centre of photo.
#     The slow drift moves toward this point.
#   crop=x0,y0,x1,y1 (0 to 1) cuts a photo out of a screenshot.
#   Clips (videos and GIFs) play muted, up to 9 s from the middle. clip=START,LENGTH (seconds) picks the part.
#   Notes starting with "?" are guesses (which face is Lino, or the date). Please check those.
#
# NEW PHOTOS land in a "NEW" chapter at the bottom until they are placed.
# After editing: .venv/bin/python build/slideshow.py
#
